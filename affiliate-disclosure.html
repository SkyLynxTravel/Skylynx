Important Disclosure

SkyLynx Travel participates in various affiliate marketing programs. This means we may earn commissions on qualifying purchases made through our links, at no additional cost to you.

How Our Affiliate Program Works

SkyLynx Travel is committed to providing honest, unbiased travel recommendations. To support our operations and continue delivering high-quality content, we participate in affiliate programs with trusted travel partners.

What Are Affiliate Links?

Affiliate links are special tracking links that allow us to earn a small commission when you make a purchase through our recommendations. These links:

  • Do not increase the price you pay for any product or service
  • Do not affect the availability or terms of the offer
  • Help support our research, content creation, and platform maintenance
  • Are clearly marked throughout our website
Your Privacy Matters

When you click on affiliate links, tracking cookies may be placed on your device to attribute the sale to our referral. These cookies typically expire within 30-90 days. For more details, please review our Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy.

Our Commitment to You

1. Honest Recommendations

We only recommend products and services that we genuinely believe will benefit our users. Our AI-powered recommendations are based on extensive research, data analysis, and when possible, personal experience.

2. Transparency

We clearly disclose our affiliate relationships throughout our platform. This page serves as our primary disclosure, and we also include brief disclosures on pages containing affiliate links.

3. Editorial Independence

Our affiliate partnerships do not influence our editorial content. We maintain complete editorial independence, and our recommendations are based solely on what we believe offers the best value and experience for our users.

Legal Compliance

This disclosure complies with Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines in the United States and similar regulations worldwide. We are committed to full transparency and legal compliance in all our affiliate marketing activities.

Understanding Commissions

Commission rates vary by partner and product type. Typical commission structures include:

  • Hotel bookings: 2-8% of booking value
  • Flight bookings: 1-4% of ticket price
  • Car rentals: 2-6% of rental cost
  • Travel packages: 3-10% of package price

These commissions help support our operations, including:

  • AI technology development and maintenance
  • Content creation and research
  • Website hosting and security
  • Customer support services
  • Continuous platform improvements

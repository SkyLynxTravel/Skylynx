About SkyLynx Travel

Ottawa-based luxury travel concierge and booking platform. Combining cutting-edge technology with personalized service to deliver exceptional travel experiences.

Our Story

From Ottawa to the World

Founded in 2023 in Ottawa, Canada, SkyLynx Travel began with a simple vision: to make luxury travel accessible, seamless, and truly exceptional. What started as a passion project between travel enthusiasts has grown into a premier travel concierge service serving clients across the globe.

Our founders, seasoned travelers themselves, recognized a gap in the market for a travel service that combined the convenience of modern technology with the personalized touch of traditional travel agencies. They envisioned a platform where discerning travelers could find everything they need in one place - from commercial flights to private jets, boutique hotels to luxury resorts.

Today, SkyLynx Travel operates from our headquarters in Ottawa while serving clients worldwide. We've built partnerships with leading travel providers across six continents, ensuring our clients receive the highest quality service at competitive prices.

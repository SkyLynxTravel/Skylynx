Our Story

SkyLynx Travel was founded by a team of seasoned aviation and hospitality professionals with over 20 years of combined experience working for world-leading airlines and luxury travel companies.

Based in Ottawa, Canada's capital, we leverage cutting-edge AI to revolutionize luxury travel. Our deep industry knowledge combined with advanced technology allows us to offer unparalleled service.

Our Mission: To make luxury travel accessible, intelligent, and unforgettable by combining decades of aviation expertise with the power of AI.

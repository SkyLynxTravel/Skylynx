Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority. Learn how SkyLynx Travel protects your personal information while delivering AI-powered luxury travel experiences.

AI-Enhanced Privacy Protection
Our Commitment to Privacy

SkyLynx Travel International Inc. ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website skylynx.travel or use our services.

Information We Collect

1. Personal Information You Provide

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number
  • Account Information: Username, password, preferences
  • Travel Preferences: Destination interests, budget range, travel dates
  • Communication Data: Messages, feedback, inquiries

2. Automated Collection

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, click patterns
  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system
  • Location Data: Generalized location (city/country level)
  • Cookies & Tracking: As detailed in our Cookie Policy

3. Information from Affiliate Partners

  • Awin Network: Click and conversion data for affiliate tracking
  • Travelpayouts: Booking confirmation and commission data
  • Other Partners: Aggregated performance analytics
Minimal Data Collection

We practice data minimization, collecting only what's necessary to provide our services. We never sell your personal information to third parties.

Affiliate Marketing & Tracking

As part of our business model, we participate in affiliate programs including:

Awin Network

Global affiliate network connecting us with premium travel brands
